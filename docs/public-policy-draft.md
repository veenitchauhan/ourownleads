# Our Own Leads — proposed public pages

These pages describe the current development beta. Contact: contact@ourownleads.com.

## /privacy — Privacy notice

Our Own Leads helps businesses organize leads, business knowledge, and WhatsApp conversations.

We store the account and business details you provide, imported lead details and custom fields, messaging-permission records, business knowledge, conversation content and delivery information. When you connect WhatsApp, we store the business and phone identifiers and authorization credentials needed for the connection. Passwords are hashed and connection credentials are encrypted in storage.

We use this information to provide your workspace, import your selected data, connect your WhatsApp business account, send messages you request, receive replies, and maintain the service. Workspace information is accessible to the workspace owner; authorized platform administrators can access information needed to operate and support the service.

WhatsApp connection and messaging information is sent to Meta as needed to provide those features. Reading an accessible Google Sheet makes a request to Google for the sheet you select. During this development beta, the application and database run on the operator’s computer, and public HTTPS traffic passes through Cloudflare Tunnel. Automated AI replies are not currently enabled.

We use session cookies for sign-in. The application does not include advertising or analytics tracking. We retain workspace data until it is removed or you request account deletion; operational records may be retained where necessary for security or legal obligations. Contact us for access, correction, deletion, or questions. Do not submit sensitive personal information to this development beta.

## /terms — Beta service terms

Our Own Leads is a development beta for business use. Features may be incomplete or unavailable, and availability is not guaranteed. Do not rely on the beta as your only copy of business records.

You are responsible for the accuracy of information you import, protecting your account, and having permission to use and message your contacts. Do not use the service for unsolicited bulk messages, impersonation, fraud, or unlawful activity. WhatsApp features are also subject to Meta’s applicable terms and messaging requirements.

You retain your rights in the information you provide and permit us to process it to provide and support the service. No paid subscription is created by signing up for this beta. Any future paid plan will be presented separately before purchase.

We may restrict use that compromises the service or other people. You can stop using the service and request deletion using the instructions below. Contact contact@ourownleads.com with questions.

## /data-deletion — Request data deletion

To request deletion of your Our Own Leads account or connected Meta data, email contact@ourownleads.com from your account email address with the subject “Our Own Leads data deletion”. Include your workspace or business name and whether you want the whole account deleted or only the WhatsApp connection and associated data removed. Do not send passwords, access tokens, or identity documents.

We will verify your ownership before removing information and confirm when the request is completed. Whole-account deletion includes the account, leads, business knowledge, conversations, and stored WhatsApp credentials. We will explain any information we must retain and why. Deleting data here does not delete messages already delivered to recipients or records held independently by Meta or other services.

You can also revoke the app’s access from your Facebook business integrations settings. Revoking access alone does not delete data already stored in Our Own Leads; send a deletion request as described above.
